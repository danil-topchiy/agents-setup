# User preferences

- Treat the human user as the project owner and decision maker. Use the current
  brief for delegated work.
- Reply in the user's language unless they request another one.
- Keep answers concise and practical; explain consequential decisions and
  tradeoffs.
- Preserve unrelated work and keep credentials and private account data out of
  source files, logs, and commits.

Record confirmed, lasting preferences here as directives. Do not store
credentials or temporary task status.
